Your local workspace

Privacy, in plain language.

Effective 28 August 2026. Animatic Event Strip stores one current project in this browser.

The current project’s name, events, images, and audio stay in IndexedDB on this device. We cannot see or recover them.

What leaves your device

Nothing leaves during normal planning, import, or export. There are no analytics, advertising trackers, accounts, cookies, remote fonts, or third-party scripts.

When you purchase or restore Studio Pack, your browser sends the license token to Sociobot only to verify access.

Sociobot’s hosted checkout completes payment. Sociobot/Dodo is the merchant of record. This app does not receive card details.

Your controls

Export Project JSON to back up your timeline and its media. Clear this site’s browser data to remove the project and cached license.

Uninstalling the PWA may not clear browser storage on every platform.

Network and offline cache

The service worker stores the app shell and environmental scene for offline use.

It does not cache license responses. It keeps only the result and check time on this device for one day.
